# Prompt for Grok — X research for LEDGE (paste the block below as-is)

---

You are doing research on X for a small independent data site called LEDGE
(ledge.tools). It indexes every token launched on **pons** — the launchpad
on **Robinhood Chain** — from the factory contract, hourly, and publishes
facts with their sample sizes: how many launches never take a buy, how long
each graduation took, what happens to a token after it graduates. It never
scores or recommends tokens. It is about to launch its own token on pons.
Search X (posts, replies, quote posts, Spaces descriptions) from the **last
45 days** and answer the questions below. Quote posts verbatim with the
handle, date and link. Prefer specific evidence over summaries. Where you
cannot find something, say "no evidence found" rather than inferring.

**1. What pons traders actually talk about.**
Search for "pons", "ponsfamily", "pons launchpad", "Robinhood Chain memecoin",
"$PONS", "pons graduated", "pons rug", "pons sniper", "pons bot". List the 15
most-engaged posts (by likes + reposts + replies) and, for each, one line on
what the poster wanted or complained about. Then group the complaints and
wishes into themes with counts.

**2. Graduation and sniping.**
Find every post in the window that mentions a pons token graduating in
seconds, being sniped in the launch block, deployer self-buys, bundled buys,
or "fake volume" on pons. How do people describe it? What words do they use?
Do any accounts publish numbers about it? Quote them.

**3. Tools people use for pons.**
Which tools, bots, screeners, Telegram bots or sites do pons traders mention
using (PonsScan, DexScreener, GMGN, Photon, BullX, Axiom, Padre, Bubblemaps,
RugCheck, custom bots, anything else)? For each: how often it is mentioned,
what for, and any praise or complaint about it. Is there anything people ask
for that no tool provides?

**4. PonsScan specifically.**
Everything said about ponsscan.xyz: who runs it, what people like or dislike,
whether it has a token, how it is promoted. Quote.

**5. Tool tokens and "utility" tokens on pons and comparable launchpads.**
Find tokens launched on pons (or pump.fun / Base launchpads in the same
window) whose pitch was a data tool, analytics site, screener or bot. For
each: the pitch in the launch post, what the token did (peak, current, dead
or alive if visible), and how the community reacted. What separated the
ones that held from the ones that died?

**6. The loud accounts.**
The 20 accounts with the most reach talking about pons or Robinhood Chain
memecoins: handle, follower count, what they post (calls, data, drama,
tooling), and whether they have ever shared a data or analytics site.

**7. The chain itself.**
What is being said about Robinhood Chain as a venue in the last 45 days:
volume, activity, whether it is growing or fading, notable launches,
anything Robinhood itself posted. Any numbers with sources.

**8. Any mention of LEDGE.**
Search "ledge.tools", "ledgetools", "LEDGE pons". Quote anything found.

**9. What would make a trader open a stats site every day?**
From everything above, infer — and label it as inference — the three things
a pons trader would return daily for, and the three things that would make
them dismiss a site in one glance.

**Format.** Markdown. Number the sections as above. Every claim with a link.
Separate "evidence" from "inference" explicitly. End with a list of the
search queries you actually ran.
