# BRAND-TRUTH

- name: LEDGE (ledge.tools)
- purpose: Publishes the measured graduation rate of every Pons launch, hourly, with every number carrying its denominator.
- core_verb: counts
- metaphor: the ledger. A ruled register where a total is written with a rule above and a double rule below. The name carries the second reading for free: a ledge is the edge a launch walks along; the ruled line is where it either holds or drops.
- palette: ink #16140E on bone #EDE9E0 (light); bone #E9E5D9 on ink #131210 (dark). No accent hue in the mark. The stale red exists on the site only.
- constraints: reads at 16px favicon and at 40px in a Pons token list; square 1024 token image; two-tone only; no gradients, glows, shadows, purple, emoji, mascots, charts, cliffs, mountains, percent signs, magnifying glasses, coins, rockets; radius 0; shared unchanged between site and token.
