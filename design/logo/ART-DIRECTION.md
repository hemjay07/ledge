# ART-DIRECTION

## Rejected literal solutions
A cliff or mountain silhouette (ledge), a rising bar chart, a percent sign, a gauge needle, a coin. All forbidden.

## Signature element: the Ruled L
Three rules taken straight from the page's own register:
1. the folio margin rule, vertical, medium weight;
2. the total rule, horizontal, heavy, meeting the margin rule at a right angle;
3. the double hairline beneath the total, the accountant's mark for "this line is the sum".

Read together they form the letter L, a ledger total, and a ledge in profile (a shelf edge with a lip beneath it). Nothing is drawn that is not already on the page, so the mark on the token is the page and the page is the mark.

## Material
Flat ink on paper. No depth, no highlight, no texture in the vector. Hard edges, radius 0.

## Composition
Mark sits in the lower-left of its square with the heavy rule running right, leaving the upper-right quadrant empty: the negative space is the ledge's drop. Generous margins so the silhouette survives at 40px.

## Wordmark
LEDGE set in IBM Plex Mono 600, tracked +0.18em, all caps. The mark can precede it or stand alone. No Anton in the wordmark: Anton is reserved for the figures on the page.

## Palette
Ink on bone. Inverted for dark surfaces. Token image: bone square, ink mark. OG variant: bone field, ink mark at left, wordmark beside it.

## Forbidden
Gradients, glows, shadows, bevels, 3D, rounded corners, any hue, any pictorial object, any chart, any letterform other than the L that emerges from the rules.

## Dual legibility
At 16px: a bold L with a lip. At 1024px: three rules with visibly different weights, exactly the weights used on the site (hairline / mid / heavy).
