# LEDGE mark

The signature element is the Ruled L: three rules taken from the site's own register. The vertical is the folio margin rule; the heavy horizontal is the total rule; the two hairlines beneath are the accountant's mark for a sum. Together they read as the letter L, a ledger total, and a ledge in profile. The empty upper-right quadrant is the drop.

Ink on bone, inverted on dark surfaces. No hue, no radius, no shadow. Shared unchanged between the site and the LEDGE token on Pons.

Wordmark: LEDGE in IBM Plex Mono 600, letter-spacing 0.18em. Anton is reserved for figures on the page and is never used in the wordmark.
