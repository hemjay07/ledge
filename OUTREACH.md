# Outreach

Ten messages, hand-sent, one at a time. This is the week's real work: the site does not spread on its own, and every figure below is one nobody else has published.

Rules for all of them. Short. No pitch. Lead with the number, not with LEDGE. Offer the data free. Never accuse a named token, wallet or person — the finding is about a population, and the moment it reads as an accusation about someone it stops being a measurement. Send from your own name.
