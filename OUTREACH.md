# Outreach

Hand-sent, one at a time, in the order below. The site does not spread on its own, and nothing in this file works if it is sent as a broadcast.

**Before you send anything: the link must resolve.** Until `ledge.tools` is registered and pointed at Vercel, substitute `ledge-alpha.vercel.app` in every message below. A dead link in the first line is the whole pitch.

## What changed, and why the order changed

The old version of this file led with the exposé — 62.9% of graduations complete inside five minutes, so the real rate is 0.66%. That message recruits readers who now believe pons launches go to zero, and then asks them to buy a token launched on pons. It also made the first thing pons ever heard from us an accusation, while our revenue sits inside their building.

The lead is now the thing they can actually use. `ponsfamily.com/analytics` renders "$0" trading volume and "0" token launches, with the note "Dune history is unavailable." A launchpad with a $551.6M-FDV token and roughly $35M of daily volume is shipping a dead statistics tab. We hold 108,317 launches and 1,932 graduations, indexed hourly from the factory contract and reproducible from public data. That is a replacement for something visibly broken, not a favour.

The fast-graduation finding does not disappear. It stays published on `/method`, where it belongs — as the reason the rate excluding fast graduations is 0.66% rather than 1.78%, and as the measured reason, and as evidence for a caveat pons wrote themselves: *"Graduation only confirms the threshold was reached. It is not a quality signal and does not guarantee future liquidity, price, or an exit."* Their words. We measured them.

## Rules for every message

Short. No pitch. Lead with the number or the offer, never with LEDGE. Give the data away free. Never accuse a named token, wallet or person — the finding is about a population, and the moment it reads as an accusation about someone it stops being a measurement. Write pons in lowercase. Send from your own name.

## The figures, as of the last measurement

Refresh from `data/number.json` before sending. Do not send a stale number to someone who will check it.

- 108,317 launches indexed from the pons launch factory, continuously, since block 55,173,069
- 1,932 graduations — 1.78%
- 0.66% excluding graduations that completed inside five minutes — 1 in 151
- 62.9% of all graduations complete inside five minutes; 39.6% inside sixty seconds
- 93.5% of launches never take a single buy (187 of 200 matured launches sampled, quote reserve read live from each launch's own bonding curve, 8 Sep 2026)
- Median graduated token: $130 of 24-hour volume — $0.91/day to its creator at the 0.7% creator share
